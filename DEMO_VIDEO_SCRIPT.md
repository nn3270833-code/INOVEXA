# MediInfo - 2-Minute Demo Video Walkthrough Script

This script is designed for recording a 2 to 3-minute video walkthrough of the **MediInfo** portal for hackathon evaluation.

---

## 🎬 Recording Setup Tips
- **Resolution:** Full HD (1920x1080)
- **Browser:** Chrome or Edge in full-screen mode (`F11`)
- **Recommended Free Screen Recorders:**
  - [Loom](https://www.loom.com/) (Instant shareable link)
  - Windows built-in screen recorder (`Win + Alt + R` or `Win + G`)
  - OBS Studio
- **Hosting Options for the Final Link:**
  - **YouTube:** Upload as "Unlisted" and copy the link.
  - **Loom:** Shareable link (ready immediately after recording).
  - **Google Drive:** Upload MP4 file, set permissions to *"Anyone with the link can view"*, and copy link.

---

## ⏱️ Scene-by-Scene Script

### Scene 1: Introduction & Interactive Canvas (0:00 - 0:25)
* **Screen Action:** Open `MediInfo_Medicine_Information_Portal FINAL.html`. Show the fluid water animation with floating tablets and capsules. Move the cursor to create ripples, click a floating pill to reveal the preview tooltip. Log in using your credentials.
* **Narration:**
  > *"Hello judges, welcome to MediInfo, an intelligent, multilingual medicine information and patient safety portal designed by Team INOVEXA. Healthcare accessibility begins with clarity and safety. Before logging in, users can interact with floating medicines on our fluid physics canvas. Behind the scenes, user accounts utilize Web Crypto SHA-256 and client-side encryption."*

---

### Scene 2: Medicine Search & Indian Brand Price Comparison (0:25 - 0:55)
* **Screen Action:** Navigate to the **Home** dashboard. Search for `Augmentin` or `Paracetamol`. Open a brand card (e.g., *Augmentin 625 Duo* or *Dolo 650*). Scroll down to the **"Cheaper alternative"** section showing percentage and rupee savings.
* **Narration:**
  > *"In India, patients often spend unnecessary money on expensive branded medicines without realizing identical generic equivalents exist. MediInfo solves this with our real-time Brand & Price comparison engine. For example, when viewing Augmentin 625, MediInfo highlights identical formulation alternatives, instantly revealing substantial savings per pack—often over 50% cheaper."*

---

### Scene 3: The "Hindsight" Safety Framework & Interaction Radar (0:55 - 1:30)
* **Screen Action:** Click on the **Hindsight** tab. Add a couple of medications to the cart (e.g., Ibuprofen + Aspirin or Warfarin). Show the **Duplicate-ingredient radar** highlighting the warning alert. Demonstrate the **Preparedness Score** bar and the **Pharmacy Handoff Card** with 1-click WhatsApp/Print.
* **Narration:**
  > *"Our flagship innovation is the 'Hindsight' Safety Framework. Instead of complex medical leaflets, Hindsight gives patients a clear timeline: what to ask before starting, what side effects to watch for during the first month, and how to taper off safely. Our built-in Duplicate-ingredient Radar automatically detects hazardous combinations—like combining NSAID painkillers with blood thinners—and generates a Pharmacy Handoff Card ready to share via WhatsApp or print."*

---

### Scene 4: MediBot AI Assistant & 18 Indian Languages (1:30 - 2:05)
* **Screen Action:** Click the floating **MediBot** icon. Ask a question via voice or text (e.g., *"Is Dolo 650 safe for children?"*). Then click the **Language Selector** at the top right and switch to Hindi, Telugu, or Tamil to demonstrate live translation across all cards and UI.
* **Narration:**
  > *"MediBot is our AI-powered multimodal assistant. It combines instant sub-millisecond local triage with safety filters that immediately redirect medical emergencies to helpline 112. Furthermore, with the click of a button, MediInfo translates across 18 Indian regional languages—including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and Kannada—ensuring critical medical precautions reach every citizen in their native language."*

---

### Scene 5: Conclusion & Future Outlook (2:05 - 2:20)
* **Screen Action:** Return to the Home screen, show the clean responsive interface, and display the GitHub repository link.
* **Narration:**
  > *"With client-side AES-256 encryption for family health profiles, prescription upload simulations, and offline-first capabilities, MediInfo empowers patients with safe, affordable, and accessible medicine intelligence. Thank you!"*

## 🔗 How to Fill the "Video Link" in the Hackathon Form

Once you have recorded the video:
1. **Loom:** Copy your loom video URL (e.g., `https://www.loom.com/share/...`)
2. **YouTube:** Upload to YouTube → set visibility to **Unlisted** → copy the YouTube URL (e.g., `https://youtu.be/...`)
3. **Google Drive:** Upload the `.mp4` video to Google Drive → Right click → **Share** → Set to **"Anyone with the link can view"** → Copy the Drive link.
