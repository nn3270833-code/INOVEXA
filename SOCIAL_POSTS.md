**Title:**
> Built MediInfo: A privacy-first medicine safety & Indian brand price comparison portal with 18 regional languages and client-side encryption

**Post Body:**
> Hey everyone,
>
> As part of our hackathon submission, our team built **MediInfo** – an open-source web application tackling medication safety and price transparency in India.
>
> ### Why we built it:
> 1. In India, people frequently overspend on branded medicines when identical generic alternatives exist at a fraction of the cost.
> 2. Many patients accidentally take duplicate active ingredients (e.g. taking Dolo 650 alongside a cold remedy containing paracetamol).
> 3. Language barriers prevent millions from understanding critical medical warnings.
>
> ### What it does:
> - **Brand vs Generic Price Intelligence:** Compares 250+ tablets & capsules with real-world Indian MRPs, calculating per-unit economics and showing cheaper alternatives.
> - **The "Hindsight" Framework:** A chronological roadmap showing patients what questions to ask before starting, what side-effects to watch for, and how to taper off safely.
> - **Duplicate-Ingredient Radar:** Client-side interaction engine flagging NSAID overlaps, blood thinner conflicts, and duplicate drugs.
> - **Multilingual UI (18 Indian Languages):** Dynamic DOM translation preserving units (mg, ml, IU) and numerals.
> - **Client-side Encryption:** Encrypted family health profiles using Web Crypto API (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM-256).
> - **MediBot AI Assistant:** Instant local triage + safety filter routing emergencies to 112.
>
> 🔗 Code & Demo: https://github.com/nn3270833-code/INOVEXA
>
> Would love your feedback and thoughts!
